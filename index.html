Peyto Lake — Bow Summit Viewpoint
45 min
⏱
Time to spend: 40–45 min — 10 min walk up to the viewpoint, 20 min taking photos and enjoying the view, 10 min back to the car.
📍
Address: Bow Summit, Icefields Pkwy, Banff NP, AB (highest point on the Parkway, 2,069 m)
🚗

Photo spot: Main railed viewpoint platform — best early morning (9 AM) for cloud-free light. The lake shape is only visible from above — this is the shot. 📸 Best view on the entire Parkway

Columbia Icefield Skywalk
1 hr
⏱
Time to spend: 45–60 min — 10 min walk to the skywalk, 20 min on the glass walkway and outer edge, 10 min reading interpretive panels, 10 min walk back. Do this first, before lunch and Ice Explorer.

📸
Photo spot: On the glass floor looking straight down, or from curved outer edge looking back at Athabasca Glacier. 📸 Photo perfect

Lunch + Free Time (12:45–3:00 PM)
🍽 Packed Lunch from Canmore + Discovery Centre Exhibits
Lunch: Eat your packed sandwiches from Communitea Café or Safeway — enjoy them at the Discovery Centre outdoor terrace or picnic area with glacier views. Far better than any cafeteria!
After lunch: You have ~1.5 hrs before your 3 PM Ice Explorer. Use this time to explore the free interpretive exhibits inside the Discovery Centre — glacial history, wildlife, climate change displays. Genuinely interesting for kids and adults.
Also onsite: Starbucks, gift shop, and clean washrooms. Good time to layer up in warm clothing for the glacier.

Columbia Icefield — Ice Explorer
2 hrs
⏱
Time to spend: 2 hrs — 30 min bus ride up to the glacier, 45 min on the ice, 30 min bus ride back, 15 min for the Discovery Centre exhibits. Don't rush on the glacier itself.

📸
Photo spot: On the glacier surface with the Ice Explorer bus behind + mountain wall ahead. Blue stake markers show the best photography zone. 📸 Photo perfect

Banff Upper Hot Springs ♨️ Optional
1–1.5 hrs
⏱
Time to spend: 1–1.5 hrs — change (10 min), soak (45–60 min), change back and pack up (10 min). Perfect way to unwind after a big glacier day.
📍
Address: 1 Mountain Ave, Banff, AB T1L 1K2
🚗
Parking: All Sulphur Mountain lots — including Hot Springs — cost CAD $17.50/day (same 2026 pilot as the Gondola). To avoid the fee, park FREE at Banff Train Station lot (Spirit Ave & Lynx St) and take Roam Bus Route 1 "Sulphur Mountain" — stops right at the Hot Springs.
🕐
Hours: Open daily 1:00 PM–10:00 PM (last entry 9:30 PM). Reopened April 2026 after renovation. Arriving at 6 PM is perfect.
🎫
Cost: CAD $17.50/adult, $15.25/youth (3–17), $56.75/family. Pay at the door — no advance booking needed. Lockers available.
👜
Bring: Pack swimsuits and towels in the car before leaving the hotel this morning.
👀
What to see: Historic outdoor hot spring pool at 1,585 m with mountain ridge views. Water is 37–40°C. Kids love it like a warm outdoor pool.
📸
Photo spot: From inside the pool looking up at Sulphur Mountain ridge — especially beautiful in evening light. 📸 Photo perfect

Vermilion Lakes — Sunset View 🌅
45 min
⏱
Time to spend: 40–45 min — arrive 30 min before sunset (~9:15 PM in early June). No walking needed — just pull up and watch from the car or roadside.
📍
Address: Vermilion Lakes Dr, Banff, AB — turn west off Banff Ave just before the Hwy 1 on-ramp
🚗
Parking: Free pullouts along Vermilion Lakes Dr. No fee. Drive slowly along the lake road and pull off anywhere with a clear view west.
👀
What to see: Three calm shallow lakes with Mt. Rundle and Sulphur Mountain reflecting in the water. Elk frequently graze at dusk. Sunset turns the mountains pink and orange — one of the best free views in all of Banff.
🦌
Wildlife tip: Pull up slowly and quietly — elk are often right at the roadside. Stay in or near your car. Do not approach.
📸
Photo spot: Face southwest from the first or second pullout — Mt. Rundle reflected in still water with the sunset sky behind it. 📸 Best sunset shot in Banff

Spiral Tunnels Viewpoint Optional
